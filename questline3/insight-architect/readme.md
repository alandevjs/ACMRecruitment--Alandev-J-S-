Insight Architect
This project performs Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) on the Students Performance Dataset using Python.

Visualizations
The following visualizations were created:

Histogram of math score distribution
Bar chart comparing average math scores by gender
Box plot comparing math scores based on test preparation
Scatter plot showing the relationship between math and reading scores
Heatmap showing correlations between math, reading, and writing scores
Insights
Most of the students scored between 50 and 80 in math, with many scores around 60–70.

The male students had a higher average math score than the female students.

Students who completed the test preparation course had a higher median math score than students who did not complete it.

Students with higher math scores usually also had higher reading scores.

Math, reading, and writing scores all have a positive relationship.

Reading and writing had the strongest relationship, with a correlation of 0.95.

Math and writing had the weakest relationship of the three, with a correlation of 0.80.
